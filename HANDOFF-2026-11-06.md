## Measured numbers (BENCHMARK-2026-11-05.md is the source)
- Own 30-URL benchmark: 26/30 fetched, 92/94 fidelity checks (97.9%), median 1,454 ms, median memory 56.4 MB.
- Versus Firecrawl free keyless tier, 24 URLs with references, mostly hand-scored, one run each: 91/93 vs 85/93; we score higher on 6, tie 18, lower 0.
- Coverage: Firecrawl returned content for 30/30; we fetched 26/30. The 4 misses are deliberate: text.npr.org and reuters.com (robots.txt `Disallow: /` for all agents), zillow.com and glassdoor.com (HTTP 403 to our declared user agent). No engine defect.
