Where we differ

What we do not do

We do not rotate proxies, disguise requests or solve CAPTCHAs. A site behind a bot wall stays refused on the live read, and you receive a dated archive copy instead. If your work depends on getting through bot walls at volume, ScrapingBee is the better fit.
