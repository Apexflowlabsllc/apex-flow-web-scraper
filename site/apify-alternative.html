Is it cheaper than Apify?

It depends on the Actor. Apify sells platform usage in dollars and each Actor sets its own price per result or per compute unit, so there is no single per-page figure to compare. Our price is fixed: 1 credit for a plain page, from $0.14 down to $0.08 per 1,000.

Where we differ

  • A one-time pass. $5 for 35,000 credits, valid 30 days, no subscription and no renewal.
  • No overage charges. When credits run out, requests stop. Failed scrapes return their credits.
  • Rendered pages cost 3 credits. Apify bills compute units and per-result prices set by each Actor, so a rendered page has no single price.
  • Change tracking and token-budget reads are included. Every read says whether the page is new, unchanged or changed, and max_tokens sizes a page to your model.
  • A labelled archive copy when a site refuses. You get the latest Internet Archive copy with its capture time instead of an error, unless you switch that off.
  • Open source engine (MIT). Run the same converter yourself, including a reader for pages you are signed in to, on your own machine.

Where Apify is ahead

  • A marketplace of thousands of ready-made scrapers for specific sites. We have a general reader and a small set of data packages.
  • $5 of free platform usage every month.
  • A full platform for scheduling, storage and custom code that we do not try to match.

What we do not do

We do not rotate proxies, disguise requests or solve CAPTCHAs. A site behind a bot wall stays refused on the live read, and you receive a dated archive copy instead. If your work depends on getting through bot walls at volume, Apify is the better fit.
