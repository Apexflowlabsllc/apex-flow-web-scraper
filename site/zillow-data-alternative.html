Data packages

Housing data for any US metro, in one call.

Zillow's terms forbid automated reading and reuse of its pages, so scraping it is a dead end for a product. Our home package answers the same questions from data you may use and resell: the house price trend, monthly rent for a studio to a 4-bedroom, and this week's mortgage rates.

curl https://webscraper.apexflowlabs.com/v1/packages/home \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APEX_SCRAPER_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"region":"Phoenix, AZ"}'

Part of the reply (rents, real data)

Questions

Can I scrape Zillow with it?

A Zillow page you name is fetched like any page, but Zillow's terms forbid automated use and reuse, and it often refuses automated readers. The home package gives you the numbers people go to Zillow for, from sources whose terms allow it.

Is this a Zestimate?

No. The house price figure is an index of how prices in the metro moved (compare periods), not a value for one house. Rents are dollar figures by bedroom count.

What does it cost?

One credit an answer: about $0.00014 on the $15 Starter plan. A list of possible place names, when your question matches more than one, is free.
