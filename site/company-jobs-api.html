Data packages

Every open job at a company, straight from its own board.

LinkedIn and Indeed wall off their listings and forbid scraping. Most tech companies publish their open roles on a Greenhouse or Lever job board that is offered as a public feed for anyone to read, and the jobs package reads that feed: title, team, location, link, and pay where the company lists it.

curl https://webscraper.apexflowlabs.com/v1/packages/jobs \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APEX_SCRAPER_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"company":"stripe","query":"engineer","location":"New York"}'

Part of the reply (real board, read 22 November 2026)

{
  "company": "stripe",
  "board": "greenhouse",
  "open_jobs_total": 777,
  "jobs": [
    {
      "title": "Abuse Investigator",
      "location": "Seattle, San Francisco, New York City",
      "url": "https://stripe.com/jobs/search?gh_jid=8172510"
    }
  ]
}

Endpoint: /v1/packages/jobs with company and optional query, location, limit (up to 100). MCP tool: company_jobs. Pair it with the pay package for what the job pays in that city. Every answer names its source and attribution.

Questions

Which companies work?

Any company whose careers page runs on Greenhouse or Lever, which covers a large share of tech companies. Use the name in the board address, such as "stripe" in boards.greenhouse.io/stripe.

Does it read LinkedIn or Indeed?

No. Those sites forbid scraping. This reads the company's own public board, which is where those sites copy listings from.

What does it cost?

One credit when jobs come back. A name with no board, or a filter with no matches, costs nothing.
