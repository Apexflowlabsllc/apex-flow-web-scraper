Is it cheaper than ScraperAPI?

On the plans we compared, yes for plain pages: $0.14 per 1,000 on our $15 Starter against $0.49 on ScraperAPI's $49 plan, and $0.08 on our $299 Scale against $0.10 on its $299 plan.

Where we differ

  • A one-time pass. $5 for 35,000 credits, valid 30 days, no subscription and no renewal.
  • No overage charges. When credits run out, requests stop. Failed scrapes return their credits.
  • Rendered pages cost 3 credits. ScraperAPI charges additional credits for rendered and protected pages; see its documentation for the multipliers.
  • Change tracking and token-budget reads are included. Every read says whether the page is new, unchanged or changed, and max_tokens sizes a page to your model.
  • A labelled archive copy when a site refuses. You get the latest Internet Archive copy with its capture time instead of an error, unless you switch that off.
  • Open source engine (MIT). Run the same converter yourself, including a reader for pages you are signed in to, on your own machine.

Where ScraperAPI is ahead

  • Managed proxy rotation and anti-bot handling. We do not offer proxies or solve challenges.
  • 20 to 500 concurrent threads on its plans against our 6 to 20.
  • A 7-day trial with 5,000 credits (ours is 1,000 credits, once).

What we do not do

We do not rotate proxies, disguise requests or solve CAPTCHAs. A site behind a bot wall stays refused on the live read, and you receive a dated archive copy instead. If your work depends on getting through bot walls at volume, ScraperAPI is the better fit.
