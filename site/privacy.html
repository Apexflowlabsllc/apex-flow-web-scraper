Apex Flow Web Scraper

Privacy & your data

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy describes data used by the hosted Apex Flow Web Scraper service.

Account and billing data

We store your checkout email, plan, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, billing-period status and usage counts. Stripe processes payment details; our scraper service does not receive or store your full card number. API keys are stored as hashes. An access-delivery secret lets the service reproduce your key only after verifying your checkout and browser credential.

Requests and content

We process the public URLs you submit and the returned page content to perform scraping and caching. The extraction engine may temporarily cache source responses on disk. Do not submit credentials, private documents or sensitive personal information in URLs. We index limited text from up to your latest 100 successfully scraped URLs for your account’s saved-page search. Technical logs include account identifiers, request timing and operational errors. Anonymous daily counts of page views, checkout creation and activation help us measure the signup funnel. Requests are sent to the source website, which may record access.

Cookies and abuse prevention

We set a secure, HttpOnly checkout-access cookie lasting up to 30 days so the browser that started checkout can retrieve the resulting key. The dashboard keeps your API key in the current tab’s memory and does not save it to local storage. We retain a keyed hash of the request IP to limit abuse and repeat free trials. Stripe may set its own cookies during checkout and billing management.

Service providers

Vercel delivers the website and API gateway. DigitalOcean hosts the extraction service and its account database. Stripe handles checkout, subscriptions and invoices. Each receives the information needed to provide its part of the service. Hosted scraping does not send page content to an AI model provider.

Retention and your choices

Account, billing and usage records are retained to deliver access, resolve billing issues and maintain necessary business records. Cache entries expire according to the engine’s cache policy, though disk removal may occur later. To request account access, correction or deletion, contact support@apexflowlabs.com from your checkout email. Some billing records may need to be retained. Do not send your API key or payment-card details.
