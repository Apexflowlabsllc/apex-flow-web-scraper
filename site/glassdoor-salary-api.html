Data packages

Pay for any job, in any US metro.

Glassdoor sits behind a bot wall and its terms forbid scraping. Our pay package returns pay for about 800 jobs in every US metro from the government's own employer survey, which you may use and resell: median, mean, the 10th to 90th percentile and how many people do the job.

curl https://webscraper.apexflowlabs.com/v1/packages/pay \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APEX_SCRAPER_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"job":"software developer","region":"Seattle, WA"}'

Questions

Is this the same as Glassdoor salaries?

No. Glassdoor collects reports from employees; this is a survey of about 1.1 million employers run by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. It covers every metro and is not limited by how many people posted.

How current is it?

May 2024, the latest release. The government publishes a new year every spring and we update the table when it does.

What does it cost?

One credit an answer. A list of possible metro names, when your question matches more than one, is free.
