Our measured extraction comparison

On the existing 24-page reference subset, Apex passed 91 of 93 fidelity checks and Firecrawl’s free keyless tier passed 85 of 93. Apex scored higher on 6 pages and tied on 18. This was one run with hand-scored references, not a general ranking of either service.

Coverage is a separate result: Apex fetched 26 of the 30 benchmark URLs; Firecrawl returned 30. We did not fetch two robots-disallowed sources and two sites that blocked our declared agent. We do not bypass those restrictions.

Where another service may fit better

Choose a provider with higher published concurrency if you need large bursts: Apex currently allows two concurrent scrapes per account. Firecrawl offers hosted crawl, search and browser-interaction products. ScrapingBee offers proxies and geotargeting. Tavily offers open-web search and research. Apex’s saved-page search covers your own results, and whole-site crawl jobs and model extraction are currently self-hosted features.
