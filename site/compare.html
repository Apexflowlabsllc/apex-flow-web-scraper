Where another service may fit better

Apex’s hosted API includes whole-site crawl jobs, CSS extraction, paid-plan schema extraction, and search over pages already saved to your account. Account concurrency is 6 on Starter, 12 on Growth, and 20 on Scale; the service runs at most 20 active scrapes and two browser renders at once. Whole-web search requires an operator-configured provider. Apex supports bounded click/wait/scroll actions in a single rendered request, but not persistent browser-interaction sessions or managed proxy/geotargeting. Firecrawl currently offers higher concurrency (up to 100), whole-web search, browser interactions, AI-agent workflows, and enterprise controls. ScrapingBee offers proxies and geotargeting; Tavily offers open-web search and research.
