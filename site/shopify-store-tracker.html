Store watch

Track any Shopify store. See what moves.

Name a store. We save a snapshot of its public catalog. Ask again later and you get exactly what changed: new products, price changes, sell-outs, restocks. Then ask which products are moving.

1. Take a snapshot

curl https://webscraper.apexflowlabs.com/v1/stores/track \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"store":"example-store.com"}'

The first reply is the baseline. Every later reply lists the changes since your last snapshot:

{"credits_used": 1,
 "result": {"store": "https://example-store.com", "products": 56, "first_snapshot": false,
   "changes": {"new": 2, "price_drop": 1, "sold_out": 1, "activity": 9},
   "events": [{"title": "Rope Toy", "kind": "sold_out", "detail": "all variants unavailable"},
              {"title": "Travel Bowl", "kind": "price_drop", "detail": "24.00 -> 19.00"}]}}

2. Ask what is rising

curl https://webscraper.apexflowlabs.com/v1/stores/rising \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"store":"example-store.com","days":7,"limit":10}'

Products come back ranked by the changes seen in the window, each with its price, stock state, link and the signals behind its score. This call is free.

What you can do with it

  • Watch five competitors and see which new products they keep in stock and which they drop.
  • Catch a competitor's price cut the day it happens.
  • Spot products that sell out and come back: demand you can measure.
  • Give an AI agent the tools track_store and rising_products through our MCP server.

What it does not do

  • It does not report revenue or order counts. Those are not public, and a guess is not a fact.
  • "Activity" means a variant record changed between two snapshots. That happens on a sale and on any edit, so it is a signal, not a sales count.
  • It reads only the catalog file the store publishes, obeys robots.txt, and never disguises a request.
  • The ranking needs at least two snapshots taken at different times. One store can be read once an hour.
